How to Use the Website

1. Authentication (Login / Sign Up)

  • Sign Up: New users must register using a unique Username, Email, and Password. Click the "Don't have an account? Sign up" link on the main card.
  • Login: Once registered, simply enter your Email and Password to securely access the system.
  • Password Recovery: If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password?". You must verify both your Email and Username to reset it securely.

2. Home Screen Dashboard

  • Navigation Menu: The central hub of the application. Select "Find a Route & Compare" to access the map planner.
  • Theme Engine: Click the "🎨 Theme" button to dynamically change the entire website's visual color scheme. Your choice is automatically saved!
  • Logout: Securely sign out of your account by clicking the Logout button, taking you back to the authentication screen.

3. Route Finding & Comparison (By Node)

Select Nodes: Use your mouse to Click anywhere on the Map to set your Source and Destination points. You can also manually type exact Node IDs.

Pick an Algorithm: Choose from Bidirectional Dijkstra, Standard Dijkstra, A* Search, or BFS.

Find Route: Click "Find Route" to calculate the optimal path in milliseconds and draw it on the map.

Compare All Algorithms: Run a benchmarking test to compare speed and efficiency with dynamic charts and an Academic Summary.

4. Find Route by Place Names

Place Search: Type any location in Islamabad (Sectors, Landmarks, Streets). Select from the live suggestions for perfect GPS accuracy.

Multi-Stop Navigation: Click "+ Add Intermediate Stop" to add extra waypoints! You can visit multiple sectors or landmarks in a single journey.

Intelligent Snapping: Our AI backend automatically "snaps" your selected places to the nearest road node in our professional Islamabad graph.

Benchmark Places: Use the "Compare All" button to see which algorithm finds the most efficient path between your selected real-world locations.
